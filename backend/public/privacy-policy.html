Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Wedpilot ("we", "our", "the app") is a wedding planning app connecting couples, vendors, and administrators. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how it's used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and role (couple or vendor) when you register — either directly or via Google Sign-In.
  • Google Sign-In: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google to create or match your account. We never receive your Google password, and we don't request access to your Google Drive, Contacts, or any other Google service.
  • Wedding planning data: guest lists and names you add, budget and expense entries, invitation details, RSVP responses, and planning checklist items — all entered voluntarily to use the app's features.
  • Vendor data: business details, service listings, pricing, and media uploaded by vendor accounts.
  • Messages and bookings: messages exchanged between couples and vendors within the app, and booking/inquiry records.
  • Uploaded photos: invitation background images and vendor portfolio images you choose to upload.

How we use this information

  • To operate core features: guest/RSVP management, budgeting, vendor discovery and booking, messaging, and invitations.
  • To match couples with vendors, including AI-generated explanatory notes for vendor recommendations. The AI service only ever sees vendor listing data and the couple's stated preferences (budget tier, location, style) — never guest lists, messages, or contact details of real people.
  • To send account-related email (password resets, email verification).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (e.g. rate-limiting).

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information. We don't share guest lists, messages, or budget data with anyone outside your own wedding planning (i.e. the vendors you choose to contact).

Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account from within the app's settings at any time. This deactivates the account and removes personally identifying information from it; records tied to a completed booking or vendor feedback may be retained in a de-identified form for the other party's records.
